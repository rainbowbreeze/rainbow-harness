# Writing (`writing/`) — Directory Resolver

> **Primary Home for:** Long-form prose, essays, drafts, thought pieces, and philosophical writings.

---

## 1. What Goes Here
- Developed essays, blog posts, speeches, manifestos, book chapter drafts.
- Slug convention: `topic-or-title.md`

## 2. What Does NOT Go Here
- Short, distilled conceptual definitions [`concepts/`](../concepts/README.md)
- Product feature specs [`projects/`](../projects/README.md)

---
